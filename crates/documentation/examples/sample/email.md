The fastest, smartest email client. The best of Superhuman, Gmail, and Outlook in one keyboard-first inbox.

## Multi-account inbox

Have more than one address? Add additional Gmail or Google Workspace accounts in **Settings**, and they all flow into a single unified inbox.

## Signal and Noise

Emails land in one place, split into **Signal** (needs your attention) and **Noise** (everything else). Work the list with `j` / `k`, preview with `space`, and clear items with `e`.

## Shared inboxes

Give your whole team access to support@ or sales@ — assignments, internal notes, and collision detection included.
